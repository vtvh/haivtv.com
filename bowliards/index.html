❓ How to Score

Bowliards applies bowling scoring rules to pocket billiards. 10 frames, max 300 points.

Each Frame

Rack 10 balls in a triangle. You get 2 innings (chances) to pocket all 10.

⚡ Strike — run all 10 on 1st inning / Spare — clear all 10 across 2 innings – Open — didn't clear all 10

Scoring

Same as bowling:

• Strike = 10 + next 2 balls
• Spare = 10 + next 1 ball
• Open = ball1 + ball2 only

10th Frame

Strike = 2 bonus innings. Spare = 1 bonus inning. Max 3 balls.

Input Guide

Tap the number of balls you pocketed each inning. The app handles strikes (X) and spares (/) automatically.
